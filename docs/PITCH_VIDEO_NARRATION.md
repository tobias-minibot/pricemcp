# PriceMCP YC-style pitch narration

This is a separate investor narrative, not the hackathon product demo. The
final cut is approximately 2:25.

## Slide 1 — The company

PriceMCP is the neutral price layer for AI. Ask what something costs, and get
structured, source-backed evidence through one MCP-first interface across
products, flights, FX, and future price domains.

## Slide 2 — The problem

AI can find a price, but it cannot reliably trust one. Retailers name the same
item differently, variants get mixed, prices go stale, and shipping,
membership, seller identity, and availability disappear behind a headline
number. Existing rankings may also optimize ads or affiliates rather than agent
trust.

## Slide 3 — The insight

A price is not a number. It is an evidence object: a canonical subject, a
normalized quote, a trusted provider, conditions, freshness, confidence, and a
source that can be audited.

## Slide 4 — The product

An agent calls search price over MCP. PriceMCP resolves the exact entity, fans
in trusted sources, normalizes heterogeneous observations, applies trust and
freshness policy, and returns ranked offers with provenance intact.

## Slide 5 — Working proof

This is already live. The deployed AirPods query normalizes five retailer
observations. Production exposes seven read-only MCP tools. The snapshot holds
thirty-eight canonical products, and sixty-six automated tests pass. The flight
contract is clearly synthetic and non-bookable; without live evidence,
PriceMCP fails closed.

## Slide 6 — Architecture and moat

PriceMCP sits between agents and commerce. Source adapters feed category-aware
entity resolution, normalization, and trust policy. The compounding moat is not
a search box. It is coverage, entity graphs, normalization rules, provenance,
and observed price history.

## Slide 7 — Wedge and expansion

The initial wedge is Apple products: a finite catalog with exact variants, high
purchase intent, and recognizable merchants. Next comes the fifteen to twenty
US retailers that matter, then live travel, FX, and other domains through the
same interface.

## Slide 8 — Why now

Consumer shopping and travel destinations are excellent human products, but
agents need a neutral machine data plane. MCP adoption and AI buying workflows
create the opening for an independent price layer built for agents from day
one.

## Slide 9 — Business model

The model is usage-based infrastructure: a developer tier, paid MCP and API
volume, and enterprise plans for SLAs, policy, audit exports, and support.
Direct data partnerships improve coverage without selling ranking position.

## Slide 10 — Ask

We are looking for agent design partners, trusted data providers, and commerce
infrastructure teams that want neutral, auditable price evidence. PriceMCP can
become the price primitive every AI calls before it recommends what to buy.
