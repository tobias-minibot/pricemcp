Problem

AI can find a price.
It cannot reliably trust it.

⌁

The same product has different names, variants, sellers, and conditions.

◷

Prices go stale. Shipping, membership, availability, and tax are often hidden.

◉

Search rankings optimize ads and affiliates—not agent trust.

Agents need comparable evidence, not a plausible headline price.
Insight

A price is not a number.
It is an evidence object.

“$199”

A number without identity, seller, source, timestamp, conditions, or confidence cannot safely drive an agent decision.

Canonical item + normalized quote + provenance

PriceMCP keeps product or itinerary identity, provider trust, total, freshness, availability, conditions, match confidence, and source URL together.

This becomes a reusable contract for every AI surface.
